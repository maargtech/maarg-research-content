In Sanskrit and Hindi, maarg means a path: the way from one place to another. We chose it because it describes both what we build and how we want to build it.

## What we work on

Our first product, MAARG Intelligence, is about the paths people take through a city every day: the roads, junctions and signals that decide whether a commute takes twenty minutes or an hour, and whether an ambulance arrives in time.

## How we want to work

A path implies direction. We want to be clear about where we are going and honest about where we are: what is built, what is in pilot, and what is still a plan. You will see that on every page of this site.

Our mark carries the same idea: a road winding through a rising sun towards the horizon. Keep the long view in sight, and take each turn with care.
